how to check for divergence...

for bearish

check 2 latest tops.

if latest top is higher than the top before, and RSI shows opposite -> bearish

if latest top is lower than the top before, and RSI shows opposite -> bearish

for bullish

if latest bottom is higher than the top before, and RSI shows opposite -> bullish

if latest bottom is lower than the top before, and RSI shows opposite -> bullish
